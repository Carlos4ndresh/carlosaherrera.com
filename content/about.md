{{< lead >}}
I'm a Cloud Architect and DevOps Engineer who builds the infrastructure that lets engineering teams move fast without breaking things.
{{< /lead >}}

My work sits at the intersection of cloud architecture, platform engineering, and developer experience. I spend most of my time designing AWS environments that are secure by default, automated from the start, and built to scale — then making sure the teams using them don't have to think too hard about the plumbing.

I care a lot about getting infrastructure right: not just working, but understandable, auditable, and low-maintenance. A well-designed platform should feel invisible to the developers on top of it.

---

## What I Do

**Cloud Architecture** — Multi-account AWS environments, landing zones, networking, IAM, and security posture. I've designed platforms from greenfield to enterprise-scale.

**Infrastructure as Code** — Terraform and Pulumi are my primary tools. I prefer code that reads clearly and deploys predictably over clever abstractions that break at 2am.

**CI/CD & GitOps** — Pipeline design, ArgoCD, GitHub Actions, and the connective tissue between code commits and production deployments.

**Platform Engineering** — Developer portals, internal tooling, and the platforms that abstract away cloud complexity so product teams can stay in flow.

**Site Reliability** — Observability, alerting, incident response, and the boring operational work that keeps systems running when things go sideways.

## Background

I got into cloud infrastructure by way of operations — starting with on-prem systems before cloud was the default answer to everything. That background shapes how I think: I've seen what happens when infrastructure is treated as an afterthought, and I've also seen teams that got it right early and reaped the benefit for years.

I'm based in the US and work across time zones. When I'm not thinking about infrastructure, I'm usually reading about it, or occasionally touching grass.
